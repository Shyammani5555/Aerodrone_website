About Aerodrone Club

Aerodrone Club is a community of drone enthusiasts focused on drone building, aerial photography, racing, innovation, and modern aerodynamics. We conduct workshops, hands-on training, and real-time drone flying sessions for students who want to explore the world of UAV technology.
