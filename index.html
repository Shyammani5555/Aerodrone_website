About AERODRONE Club

AERODRONE is a student-led club that brings together enthusiasts from electronics, computer science, and mechanical backgrounds to learn and innovate in UAVs (Unmanned Aerial Vehicles). Our activities include hands-on builds, coding autopilots, competitions, community outreach, and research.

What we do

  • Design & build drones — fixed-wing, quadcopters, VTOL
  • Flight testing and data analysis
  • Workshops on electronics, flight controllers, and embedded systems
  • Collaborative projects with industry and faculty
